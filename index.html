    My path leads from the shop floor of a manufacturing company to applied research at the WZL of RWTH Aachen University. Over more than five years in consulting and research, I have co-supervised lean initiatives and more than 20 international benchmarking projects, guiding employees in workshops along the way. Today my focus is AI-driven knowledge management with knowledge graphs and large language models. At the core of my work is successful project delivery with sustainable impact that lasts beyond project completion.

    2. Biotech & Money Ltd., London

      Marketing internship

      Supported trade fair appearances in London and wrote trade articles.

    3. Jung Pumpen, Steinhagen

      Inventory Control Coordinator

      Led Kaizen workshops with seven warehouse employees, introduced 5S on four lines and set up a milk run for just-in-time supply. Afterwards, lines stopped less often because of missing material. I also redesigned workstations using PFEP and restructured the high-bay warehouse in SAP.

    4. RWTH Aachen

      2017

      Bachelor in Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering

      Specialisation in production engineering. Since 2021 I have studied alongside my work at WZL. In my bachelor thesis I developed a testing method for data-driven business models in tool making.

    5. WZL of RWTH Aachen University

      2021

      Benchmarking in tool making

      More than 20 projects with tool and die makers in Europe and South Africa. We compare process KPIs and show where a company should start. I also designed line supply layouts for clients.

    6. Research project at WZL

      2025

      ARLearn

      An AR environment intended to help production staff pass on their knowledge. In field tests the hardware reached its limits, and acceptance on the shop floor was low.

    7. RWTH Aachen and Bangkok

      2025

      Master in Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering

      Specialisation in innovation, entrepreneurship and marketing. Modules include production management and organisational development as well as design for manufacturing. In Global Innovation Processes I coordinated an international team with students from Los Angeles, Mexico City and Shanghai, and for the International Entrepreneurship project module I spent a week at Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok.

    8. SustainTool project, WZL

      2026

      Remanufacturing of tool components

      I am developing an MVP that assesses used tool components for remanufacturing. It builds on SustainTool, a project that identified an industry potential of around €105 million and 212 kt CO2e. The method has been published in the Journal of Physics.

      Production ontologies are converted into knowledge graphs automatically with Python. An LLM-based query layer on top lets people ask the graph questions in plain language. I also onboard new student assistants.

      Approach

      Hands-on in the process.Every project starts on site for me. I capture workflows directly in the process and talk to the people on the line to understand bottlenecks and waste first-hand. I learned this closeness to practice on the shop floor and sharpened it in benchmarking projects in tool making.

      Precise in the analysis.I then structure the findings and analyse them based on data, for example with process KPIs and industry comparisons. From this I derive measures that fit the specific company and are backed by the people on site. This combination of practical proximity and analytical depth is, for me, the foundation for sustainable success in implementation.
